LEAKGUARD AI — COLLEGE PROJECT

Problem:
Businesses lose money through duplicate payments, inflated supplier prices,
inventory discrepancies, unusual expenses and repeated transaction anomalies.

Solution:
A software-only AI financial intelligence layer that analyzes business data,
identifies potential leakage, calculates risk, and explains the evidence for
human review.

MVP flow:
Upload CSV → Clean data → Rule checks → ML anomaly detection →
Risk scoring → Dashboard → Investigation.

Core modules:
1. Expense/transaction leakage
2. Inventory reconciliation
3. Vendor intelligence
4. AI investigation assistant

Tech:
Python, Streamlit, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Plotly.
